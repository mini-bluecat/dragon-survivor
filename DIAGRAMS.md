# How Dragon Swarm works (a picture)

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[🐉 I move with arrows] --> B[Fire shoots by itself]
  B --> C[Monster goes pop!]
  C --> D[Grab stars ⭐ to level up]
  D --> E[Level up: pick a power]
  E --> F[Same power = stronger / RARE powers = best!]
  F --> G[Two powers at max = SUPER fusion ⭐]
  G --> H[Every 5 levels: a BOSS! 👑]
  H --> I[Beat boss = new world + GOLD 🪙]
  I --> A
```

## Hearts (5 only)

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Monster touches me] --> B[Lose 1 heart ❤️]
  C[Some monsters drop a heart] --> D[Grab it = heal]
  E[Dragon Armor 🛡️] --> F[Blocks a hit instead!]
```

> Tip: Monsters get a little stronger every world you clear, so keep buying shop upgrades! RARE powers are golden and extra strong — grab them when they pop up.
